## Overview
<div align="center" style="margin-top: 0px;">
  <img src="./static/images/teaser.jpg" alt="Teaser">
</div>
<p align="justify">
    <b>FreqGS</b> improves high-frequency detail reconstruction and produces more consistent renderings at extrapolated 
    viewpoints in forward-moving monocular street scenes. On held-out views <b>(top)</b>, our method better preserves lane 
    markings and subtle road details, yielding improved regional metric performance. At extrapolated viewpoints 
    <b>(bottom)</b>, it preserves more coherent lane geometry and sharper road–sidewalk boundaries, whereas existing 
    methods produce blurred, duplicated, or distorted structures.
</p>
